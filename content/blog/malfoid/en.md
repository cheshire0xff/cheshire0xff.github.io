### Malfoid

Once I clicked on some malfoid AI youtube video recommended to me, just to see what even it is.
Now my feed is full of it.
Malfoid is just alternative Harry Potter where Malfoy is a female that often falls in love with Harry. She is also often very 'tsundere'.

What amazes me the most is how now almost anyone with just an idea can create scenes with a quality of Hollywood movies. 
And quality is impressive, obviously not all of them but some are just perfection.
Even if you think quality is an issue all you have to do is wait. AI advances at neck-breaking pace.

### AI Slop

Sceptics say that no one is gonna be watching AI slop because we want to see human generated content.
I agree, but I think of AI generated movies but directed by humans kinda still as human generated content.
This I think is easy to argue since it's not like people that create AI scenes have an alternative path to show their ideas. Not like they can just create hollywood grade movies without AI assistance by spending millions of dollars. 

### Future

What I am looking forward the most are high quality short scenes from fantasy and sci fi books I read.
Already I see some nice beginnings of that on YT.

We can go further imagine a world where you can just paste a scene from a book and you will get hollywood grade scene just from that.

Going even further we can imagine a world where whole books can be fed to generate full length movies. 
As an avid fantasy reader this seems nice to me since good adaptations are very scarce. 
I suspect the money is the biggest issue for fantasy movies.
Often adaptations require a lot of CGI because of magic systems and/or world setting.
Almost every popular fantasy book would have a movie or TV adaptation in world like that.
